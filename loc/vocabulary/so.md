# Somali translation vocabulary (`so.json`)

See [`../vocabulary.md`](../vocabulary.md) for the glossary of terms, vocabulary entry conventions (POS, casing, multi-form syntax, anti-meaning callouts), and the meaning of each row.

Note: Somali (Af-Soomaali) is written in the Latin alphabet and is left-to-right. Technical and financial terminology uses standard Somali terms for banking and commerce, with established cryptocurrency conventions from so.wikipedia.org (Bitcoin). Brand and protocol names remain in English.
